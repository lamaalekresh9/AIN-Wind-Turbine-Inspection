Drones made blade inspection safe. Reviewing the footage is still slow.

A single inspection flight produces hundreds of close-range images per turbine. Inspectors still scan them by eye for cracks and coating damage, and small defects are easy to miss on the hundredth frame.

Saudi Arabia is building out its wind capacity under Vision 2030, so the number of blades to inspect is growing faster than the number of qualified inspectors.

How an inspection moves through Ain

The reviewer stays in control. The report can't be generated until every finding has been decided.

Step 1UploadDrone photos or a prerecorded video, tagged to a turbine and blade.
Step 2DetectThe model boxes each defect with its type and a confidence score.
Step 3MergeThe same defect seen across video frames becomes one finding.
Step 4ReviewAccept, reject, correct, or mark uncertain. Every decision is logged.
Step 5ReportAn Arabic report is written from approved findings only, with evidence images.
